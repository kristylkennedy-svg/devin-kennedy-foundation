Horn Island, Mississippi · July 4, 2026

Nolan Xavier Wells

2007 to 2026 · Ocean Springs, Mississippi

What happened to him?

Nolan went to Horn Island for the Fourth of July and never came home. Both autopsies say the cause and manner of his death are undetermined. The county has closed the case. His family has not.

Who he was

Long Live Nolan Wells

Nolan was born August 19, 2007, and grew up in Ocean Springs on the Mississippi Gulf Coast, the oldest son of Christine Wells Wonsley and Elmore Wonsley. He had two younger brothers, both football players like him. His mother called him her "sonshine."

At Ocean Springs High School he was a two-time All-Region wide receiver. After graduating in 2025 he went on to Southwest Mississippi Community College, where he wore number 16 for the Bears and was about to start his sophomore season.

His receivers coach, Earvin Moore, has described a player who listened, gave his input, kept looking for ways to get better, and could always find a way to make people laugh. Moore says what he misses most is Nolan's smile.

When the season started without him, his teammates wore white NW decals on their helmets and towels and sleeves stitched "LLNW," for Long Live Nolan Wells. His locker was never reassigned. His number 16 jersey still hangs in it, with his helmet on the shelf above.

His family says he was a strong swimmer who loved football and time on the water with friends. On August 19, 2026, supporters held a vigil on what would have been his 19th birthday.
