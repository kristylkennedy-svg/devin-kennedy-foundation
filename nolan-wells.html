Nolan Xavier Wells

What happened to him?

Nolan went to Horn Island for the Fourth of July and never came home. Both autopsies say the cause and manner of his death are undetermined. The county has closed the case. His family has not.

Who he was

Long Live Nolan Wells

Nolan was born August 19, 2007, and grew up in Ocean Springs on the Mississippi Gulf Coast, the oldest son of Christine Wells Wonsley and Elmore Wonsley. He had two younger brothers, both football players like him. His mother called him her "sonshine."

At Ocean Springs High School he was a two-time All-Region wide receiver. After graduating in 2025 he went on to Southwest Mississippi Community College, where he wore number 16 for the Bears and was about to start his sophomore season.

His receivers coach, Earvin Moore, has described a player who listened, gave his input, kept looking for ways to get better, and could always find a way to make people laugh. Moore says what he misses most is Nolan's smile.

When the season started without him, his teammates wore white NW decals on their helmets and towels and sleeves stitched "LLNW," for Long Live Nolan Wells. His locker was never reassigned. His number 16 jersey still hangs in it, with his helmet on the shelf above.

His family says he was a strong swimmer who loved football and time on the water with friends. On August 19, 2026, supporters held a vigil on what would have been his 19th birthday.

The autopsies

Two exams. No answer.

Neither autopsy found how Nolan died. Neither ruled out an accident. Neither ruled out something else.

State medical examiner

Undetermined

  • Cause and manner of death undetermined.
  • Two dark red-purple bruises under the scalp on the back of the head. How they happened was not determined. They were not fatal.
  • No skull fractures and no external scalp injuries.
  • Called drowning a diagnosis of exclusion and did not rule out accidental drowning.

Independent autopsy

Undetermined

  • Performed in Washington, D.C., by forensic pathologist Dr. Roger A. Mitchell Jr., with no ties to Mississippi law enforcement. Funded by Colin Kaepernick.
  • Red discoloration on the back of the skull, cause undetermined. The family's team later described blunt-force injuries to the back of the head and back.
  • No bone fractures or deep tissue injuries.
  • Could not rule out non-accidental factors.

Why it stayed unclear

Limits

  • Decomposition after two days in the heat and water.
  • The state autopsy had already removed his lungs and neck structures, so the second exam could not check them for water or neck injury.
  • The area has strong currents, according to the volunteer search group.

The friends

What the friends have said

Nolan went to Horn Island with friends he had known since high school. They have not been charged with anything, and a grand jury found no credible evidence that any of them committed a crime. We do not name them here. This is their side, as reported, next to the questions Nolan's family still has.

The groupWho he was with

One friend described the group to ABC News as diverse and tight-knit. Witnesses told authorities the party on the island grew rowdier through the afternoon and that Nolan had been drinking. Whether he was intoxicated has not been established.

Their accountWhy he stayed behind

The friends say Nolan chose to stay on the island and ride back with a different group. One friend said he stayed because of a young woman he had met. The family's view: his parents and another friend say leaving his group was out of character for him.

Their accountWhy his phone came back without him

One friend said phones are routinely left together on the boat dash at island parties, and that Nolan's was one of about 15 there. The family's view: Crump has questioned whether Nolan would have stayed on the island without it.

Around 4 p.m. a caller told Sea Tow that everyone was aboard the boat. GPS shows it left the island at 4:31 p.m. and docked on the mainland around 6 p.m. A friend called Nolan's parents around 11 p.m. The Coast Guard says it got a call from a friend around then that did not require its help.

Their accountThe viral video

The friend who filmed the argument says the voice is his own, in a separate dispute, and that Nolan was off camera. His girlfriend and uncle backed that account. Another friend said the voice was not Nolan's but could not say who was in the frame.

Why we don't name them

People have been federally charged for threatening witnesses in this case. Naming young people who have not been charged with anything puts them in danger and gives the case's critics a reason to dismiss Nolan's family. Are there suspects?

No one has been named

People ask this more than anything else. Here is what the record shows.

Law enforcementNo suspects named

The Jackson County Sheriff's Office has never named a suspect or person of interest. It said early on that it did not suspect foul play.

Grand juryNo charges

In September, a 23-member grand jury unanimously found no credible evidence that anyone who traveled to or from Horn Island that day committed a crime related to Nolan's death. The district attorney says the case is closed.

Family's legal teamTheories, not evidence

Nolan's family and their attorney have raised possibilities publicly, including that he may have left the island. No evidence for those possibilities has been released.

Federal agenciesNo announcement

The FBI, the Justice Department and the National Park Service have not announced an investigation into Nolan's death. The FBI is investigating threats made against people connected to the case.

That is why the family is asking for a federal investigation. Only investigators with subpoena power can turn questions into answers.

Understanding the case

Plain answers to common questions

What does "undetermined" mean?

It means the medical examiners could not say with confidence how Nolan died or whether it was an accident. It is not a finding of drowning, and it is not a finding of homicide. Both the state and the independent autopsy reached it.

Why didn't the autopsies just say drowning?

Drowning is what doctors call a diagnosis of exclusion. There is no single test that proves it, so it is concluded only after other causes are ruled out. Decomposition after two days, and organs already removed in the first autopsy, limited what the second exam could check.

What is a "no true bill"?

It is a grand jury's decision not to bring charges. In Nolan's case, the jury found no credible evidence that anyone traveling to or from the island committed a crime related to his death.

Can the case be reopened?

A no true bill is not an acquittal. As a general rule, prosecutors can bring new evidence to a future grand jury. The family is asking federal agencies to look for that evidence.

Why ask federal agencies instead of the state?

Horn Island is federal land managed by the National Park Service, which has its own investigators. Federal agencies can also subpoena records, like Snapchat data, that the family cannot get on their own.

What were the injuries on his head?

Both autopsies found bruising under the scalp on the back of his head. The state autopsy said it was not fatal and found no skull fracture. Neither exam determined how it happened.

Why federal

What a federal investigation could do

The county grand jury reviewed what local investigators gathered. Nolan died on land the National Park Service manages, which gives federal agencies standing to look again with tools the family does not have.

Get the records

Subpoena Snapchat and phone carrier records for July 4, so deleted or recalled messages can be checked at the source, not guessed at.

Find the witnesses

Roughly 300 people were on Horn Island that day. Collect their photos and video and pin down every sighting after 2:23 p.m.

Track every boat

Pull GPS and marine records for every vessel that left the island July 4, to answer whether anyone took him off it.

Explain the injuries

Have independent pathologists review both autopsies together and account for the bruising on the back of his head.

Tell his parents

Share what is found with Christine and Elmore Wonsley, in full, on a set timeline.
