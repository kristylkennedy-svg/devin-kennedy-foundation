How we work

Founder

Devin Kennedy

Accountability journalist. Entrepreneur. Mother.

"I started this because I kept watching the same thing happen. A child dies, a case gets closed, and the family is left reading about it in the comments. Nobody hands them the record. So we do."

Devin Kennedy is an accountability journalist whose work, published under @itsdevinkennedy, reaches hundreds of thousands of people with one rule: every claim comes from court records, verified reporting, or primary government documents. She built The Released Record, a public index of what released court files establish about named public figures, and brings that same discipline to every case the foundation takes on.

Before journalism she built and ran businesses, and she still does. That is where the foundation's impatience comes from: a problem gets a plan, a plan gets a deadline, and the people in power get a phone number.

She founded The Devin Kennedy Foundation in 2026 to put that work behind the people systems overlook, starting with the families in Mississippi who are still waiting for answers.
