The record

What is documented

Every fact below comes from news reporting, official statements or court filings, and each one links to its source. We do not claim to know how anyone died. We are asking for the evidence that would let their families know.

Kohen Wiley

Senatobia, Mississippi

1year old

Officer cleared Oct. 19 · family disputes

On June 14, 2026, Senatobia police answered a call accusing two women of stealing diapers from Walmart. As the women drove away, his mother lifted Kohen up to show officers there was a child in the car. One officer fired into the car. Kohen, sitting in his mother's lap, was killed. The driver was wounded.

The Mississippi Bureau of Investigation said the car drove toward officers and almost struck one. The family's attorney released a photo of the car's front side window shot out, which he says shows officers were not in its path. Neither woman has been charged, and officials have not released evidence of shoplifting. His mother says state investigators never interviewed her.

On October 19, Attorney General Lynn Fitch announced the officer's use of force was justified, without releasing details. His grandmother says the family learned of the decision on Facebook. Body camera and store surveillance video had not been released.

What we are asking for: release of all body camera, dash camera and Walmart surveillance video, and the full investigative file, to Kohen's family and the public.

Sources: Mississippi Free Press, Mississippi Today, CNN

Demartravion "Trey" Reed

Grenada, Mississippi
Delta State University, Cleveland

21years old

Ruled suicide · family disputes

Trey had been a student at Delta State for about a month. On the morning of September 15, 2025, he was found hanging from a tree near the campus pickleball courts. The Bolivar County coroner and the Mississippi state medical examiner both ruled his death a suicide within days and said they found no injuries consistent with an assault.

His family called for an independent investigation and a second autopsy, which was funded through Colin Kaepernick's Know Your Rights Camp. Nearly a year later, the Mississippi Free Press reported that the family says it never received the final results of that autopsy.

What we are asking for: release of all campus and city video, the full state autopsy, and the independent autopsy findings to Trey's family.

Sources: Mississippi Today, NPR, Mississippi Free Press

Tasia Fortune

Jackson, Mississippi

29years old

Case open

On August 3, 2026, Tasia was found hanging from a tree behind a vacant home in a predominantly Black neighborhood of Jackson after neighbors called police. As of late August, hers was reported as the one Mississippi hanging case of 2026 still open.

What we are asking for: a full, independent investigation and regular public updates to her family.

Sources: Baller Alert, 2026 in Mississippi

Aderrien Murry

Indianola, Mississippi

11years old

Survived · shot by police

In May 2023, Aderrien called 911 for help. A responding Indianola police officer shot him in the chest. He survived. His family asked for the officer's firing and the release of body camera footage, which the Mississippi Bureau of Investigation held while it reviewed the shooting.

His case is why we include children who survived. Protecting kids means being accountable when the people sent to help them hurt them.

Source: NPR

Our demands

What Mississippi owes these families

None of this requires anyone to accept a theory. It only requires the state to show its work.

Independent review of every disputed death

When a Black young person is found hanging or dies in unclear circumstances, an investigator with no ties to the local department reviews the case.

Evidence shared with families

Families receive the autopsy report, toxicology, and all video within a set deadline, without having to sue for it.

No final ruling in days

No manner-of-death ruling until toxicology, video and witness interviews are complete and documented.

Federal civil rights review

The U.S. Department of Justice opens a civil rights review of these cases, as Rep. Bennie Thompson has requested.

A public case tracker

The state publishes each case's status, the agencies involved, and the date of every ruling.

Body cameras released

When police harm a child, footage goes to the family promptly. Video of Kohen Wiley's shooting still had not been released when the officer was cleared four months later.
